Who We Are

We are a boutique energy law advisory firm offering exclusive tier 1 legal, and strategic advisory services to clients across the energy and natural resources value chain.

We work at the intersection of law, regulation, and commercial operations, supporting government bodies, private sector entities, infrastructure investors, and project developers operating across oil and gas, power, mining, and maritime segments. Our value lies in our industry-specific insight, transactional fluency, and policy awareness, allowing us to provide not just legal advice, but commercially sound guidance tailored to Nigeria's evolving energy landscape.

June 29-30, 2026

The flagship industry engagement initiative of the Nigerian Midstream and Downstream Petroleum Regulatory Authority, designed to engage directly with chief legal officers, general counsels, and in-house legal teams of licensed operators.
