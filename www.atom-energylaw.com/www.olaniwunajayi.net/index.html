THE FIRM

About Us

A leading corporate law firm with over 60 years of experience, renowned for advising on complex, high-stakes transactions across key sectors. We provide expert legal counsel and dispute resolution services, delivering innovative solutions to our clients' most challenging legal issues. At Olaniwun Ajayi LP, our strength lies in a team of nearly 200 highly skilled lawyers, many of whom are recognized by global legal directories such as Chambers Global, IFLR 1000, and Legal 500 as leading practitioners, Hall of Famers, and Star Partners.

With a strategic presence in key Nigerian cities and London, we are firmly rooted in the Nigerian and broader African economies. Through our robust network of partner firms, we journey with our clients across Africa, ensuring that wherever you go, from Nigeria to the rest of the continent, we are with you—delivering seamless, integrated legal solutions to navigate both local and international challenges.
